ACCEPT

The six decisions in `4f9f68c` hold. CSI refuses, with exit 2, no end-of-file block and no index, when the output does not open with `##fileformat=VCF`, and TBI still matches `tabix -p vcf`. A machine error (`-c e`, `NO_HEADER`) stays that error: the text matches the run without `-W`, and no index is written. The first 16 bytes are taken across blocks; on real output they sit in the first block, including after a 2-byte `#` line, because the rest of the header is `#` lines. A `<stem>.csi` that htslib 1.24 reads before the new `.tbi` is refused and left in place (`o.vcf.csi`, `d.csi` for `d.x/out`, `x.csi` for `-o x.csi`); a CSI run is not hidden by that name, and a dangling symlink is not. `stat` refuses an index name that resolves to a directory, including through a chain of symlinks, before anything is moved or emptied. Block-spanning lines, CRLF, a header longer than a block, and `END=` still match `bcftools index` and `tabix -p vcf` at `--threads` 1 and 128.
