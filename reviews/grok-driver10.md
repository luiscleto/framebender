The laws, the proof, the old-index warning, and the `-m` driver limits match decisions 1–36. The user docs do not.

1. **Should fix.** `docs/USAGE.md` lines 36–37. "CHROM, POS, ID, REF, QUAL, FILTER and FORMAT are kept, and the record's ALT is the input's ALT alone" is false of the records that are written. Reference `AAAAAAAA`, line `c1 5 . AA A,AAA`, `framebender norm -f ref.fa -m -any -c w`: the output is `c1 1 . AA A` and `c1 1 . A AA`. Say that the split keeps those fields, and that each record is then normalized as without `-m` (POS, REF, ALT, case, and END).

2. **Should fix.** `docs/USAGE.md` lines 52–53 and the expected-count sentence in `docs/LAWS.md` line 105. Both describe the G count as the copies the sample's GT names, including for INFO. INFO `Number=G` is the diploid count only. Two ALTs, `IG=1,2,3`, `GT=1`: `BAD_COUNT … not split`, and the line stays `C,G`. The same line with `IG=1,2,3,4,5,6` splits to `IG=1,2,3` and `IG=1,4,6`. bcftools also requires 6. Say that INFO G is diploid, and that sample G follows that sample's GT.

3. **Should fix.** `docs/USAGE.md` lines 170–171. "G counts follow the sample's GT (one copy, or two)" leaves out the two cases a caller hits. No GT key is diploid only: FORMAT `PL` and `PL=1,2,3` with two ALTs is `BAD_COUNT … not split`, while bcftools writes `PL=1,2` and `PL=1,3`. A lone `.` accepts either count: `GT:PL` `.:1,2,3` and `.:1,2,3,4,5,6` both split. `docs/LAWS.md` line 117 already says this. Add both to the USAGE differences.

4. **Should fix.** `docs/USAGE.md` lines 162–165. "bcftools drops undefined tags silently" does not match a header line whose Number is missing or not A, R, G, a count, `.`, or `P`. `##INFO=<ID=NN,Type=String,Description="no number">` and `NN=x,y` is `UNDEFINED_TAG … INFO/NN … not split`; bcftools copies `NN=x,y` onto both records. `Number=LR` with `LAD=1,2` is the same. A flag with no header line, `XY`, is copied and split; bcftools 1.24 aborts. A tag with no header line is dropped by bcftools after a warning, not silently. State those three bcftools behaviours separately.

5. **Should fix.** `docs/USAGE.md` and `docs/LAWS.md` never mention a VCF 4.4 leading phase. `GT=|0|1` with `-m -c w` is `BAD_GT … not split` and the multiallelic line is kept. Without `-m` the same line is kept. bcftools exits 255 (`Couldn't read GT`). Decision 16 said to document this. Add that row.
